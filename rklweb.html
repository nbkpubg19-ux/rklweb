The Science of Warp-Free Firing

How Synchronized Co-Shrinkage Eliminates Sanitaryware Rejections

When heavy sanitaryware pieces (WCs, washbasins, pedestals) undergo vitrification in the kiln, they shrink significantly. Conventional casting plates fail to shrink uniformly with the article, creating base friction that leads to bottom cracks, curve defects, and ovality distortion.

Pre-fired at approx. 1100°C, the R.K. Tech Half Fire Clay Setter Plate is formulated to shrink in harmony with your sanitaryware body during the firing cycle.
